The Imagination Cycle

Reusable across programmes. For each stage: three questions to ask children in the moment — conversational prompts, not a survey — and the behaviours to look and listen for when completing the observation form.

Choose

“You choose, we listen and follow your lead” “I believe my idea is worth trying”

Ask the child

  • What made you pick that over the other ideas?
  • If you could only do one thing today, what would it be?
  • Is there something you changed your mind about?

Look for

  • Makes a decision without being told what to do
  • Can give a reason for the choice, even a simple one
  • Changes their mind and can say why

Ask

“We ask, because your ideas matter to us” “I'm curious enough to watch and wonder”

Ask the child

  • What do you wonder about this?
  • What do you think will happen if you try that?
  • Is there anything you're still not sure about?

Look for

  • Asks questions without being prompted
  • Watches or studies something before jumping in
  • Makes a guess or prediction

Build

“We build it together, your ideas in our hands” “I'm ready to have a go”

Ask the child

  • How are you putting your idea together?
  • What's the tricky bit?
  • Who are you building this with?

Look for

  • Hands-on, trying things out
  • Keeps going after something doesn't work first time
  • Works alongside or with another child

Do

“We do it, turning your ideas into action” “I belong here, so I take the lead”

Ask the child

  • What did you decide to do next?
  • Did you lead this bit, or did someone else?
  • What would you do differently if you did it again?

Look for

  • Takes the initiative / leads a task
  • Carries an idea through to a finished result
  • Takes responsibility for how it turned out

Show

“We show you the difference you've made” “I tell others my views and help others use their imagination”

Ask the child

  • What do you want to tell people about what you made?
  • What would you tell a friend who wants to try this?
  • What are you proud of?

Look for

  • Explains or presents their work to someone else
  • Helps another child with theirs
  • Expresses pride or reflects on how they've changed

Week-by-week guide

Week 0 — Co-design

Flexes with child voice

Sharing activity ideas, gathering preferred choices.

Measures

Using images and cardboard, create images of activity and gather opinions using dot stickers.

Questions to open with

  • What was your favourite activity today?
  • What have you made today that you liked?

Things to consider

This is before the longer term programme with children, so there may be hesitancy to join in, as well as being busy because of 3 activities running at once.

Watch for

What activities are more popular, but also what engages children for longer lengths of time, and what children make, as well as they're verbal or physical feedback (through observation).

Week 1 — Choose & Ask

Flexes with child voice

Getting to know the group, establishing baseline.

Measures

Run the DAT, the 5-question confidence survey, and an intro qualitative chat before or alongside the making activity — not instead of it.

Questions to open with

  • What would you like to make over the next few weeks?
  • What have you made before that you're proud of?

Things to consider

This is also when children are meeting the space/facilitators for the first time — some hesitancy at Week 1 is normal and not necessarily a low "Ask/Choose" score. Note context on the observation form.

Watch for

Who picks an idea quickly vs who needs more time or reassurance — useful signal for how to pair or support them in Week 2.

Week 2 — Build

Flexes with child voice

Getting hands-on, forming working groups.

Measures

No repeat survey — facilitator observation form only, with the Build prompts above woven into conversation while children are working.

Things to consider

This is often where friction shows up — a design doesn't work, a group disagrees. Capture it, don't smooth it over; struggling productively is itself evidence of the Build stage, not a bad session.

Watch for

If the group has clearly split into sub-teams or roles, note it — it'll help you read the Week 3–4 Do data.

Week 3 — Do

Flexes with child voice

Ownership and problem-solving.

Measures

Facilitator observation form, with Do prompts front and centre — this is the week to notice who's leading, who's stepped back, and whether that's shifted from Week 2.

Things to consider

If a child's idea has changed direction since Week 1, that's not drift to correct — ask them about it; it may be the clearest sign of Choose and Build working as intended.

Watch for

Start a light mention that Week 4 will include sharing what they've made, so Show doesn't land as a surprise.

Week 4 — Show (endline)

Flexes with child voice

Sharing, reflecting, and the repeat measures.

Measures

Repeat the DAT and the 5-question confidence survey.

Exit chat / showcase

  • What do you want to tell people about what you made?
  • What would you tell a friend who wants to try this?
  • What's changed since Week 1?

Things to consider

Run the repeat survey before the showcase itself, so excitement/adrenaline from presenting doesn't skew the confidence scores. Do the "what's changed" conversation after, when they're reflecting rather than performing.

Watch for

With only 16 children, consider picking 2–3 for a short case-study writeup rather than trying to generalise from the group average alone.

This is a guide, not a script — the cycle follows the child, not the other way round.
